The family ledger

Thirty-four years of one family's kitchens.

1991

Atta Khan arrives from Pakistan

He cooks as a chef in Westchester, then brings his whole family over in 2000.

2011

Moghul opens in Vestal

A fine-dining Indian restaurant in University Plaza. It works.

2014-16

One kitchen becomes four

Binghamton University's student union, then Syracuse, then Stony Brook. Each son runs one.

2020

All four close

The pandemic takes every one of them. The brothers drive for Uber and work construction.

2024

They lose their father

And decide the family name should keep cooking.

Nov 2025

207 Grand Avenue

Five months of renovating, then the doors open. Moghul Halal Express is the fifth kitchen.
