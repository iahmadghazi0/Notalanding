The signature

Adeni tea, the heavy-spice way.

Ginger and cinnamon, no nutmeg, poured from a fresh pot. The Dallas Observer found it heavier on spice and less sweet than the other Yemeni teas in North Texas. Take a pistachio milk cake with it.
